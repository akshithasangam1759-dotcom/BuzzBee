🐝 BuzzBee — Music Social Platform
A full-stack music social platform where artists, creators, and music lovers come together to share vibes, discover new tracks, and connect in real time.


✨ Features
-Social feed with posts, reactions, and comments
-Real-time messaging and notifications
-Stories — share 24hr photo/video moments
-Vibey AI — your personal music assistant powered by Groq
-Trending music discovery via Jamendo
-Custom avatar builder
-Dark/Light mode

🛠️ Tech Stack
-Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
-Backend: Node.js, Express.js
-Database: MongoDB Atlas
-Real-time: Socket.io
-Media: Cloudinary
-AI: Groq (LLaMA 3.3-70b)
-Deployment: Vercel + Render
🔗 Live Demo: https://buzz-bee-five.vercel.app
